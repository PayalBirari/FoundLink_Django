Smart Matching Makes Finding Easier

FoundLink checks information such as item name, category, location and description to identify possible matches between lost and found reports.

  • Compares important item details
  • Identifies possible matches automatically
  • Sends notifications when a match is found
  • Helps users reconnect faster

SAFETY & TRUST

Stay Safe While Reconnecting

FoundLink encourages users to verify information and use safe communication practices when recovering belongings.

Protect Your Account

Never share your password or OTP with another person. Keep your account information private.

Verify the Item

Confirm item details such as appearance, location and identifying information before returning it.

Choose Safe Locations

Prefer public or trusted college locations when meeting someone to return or collect an item.
