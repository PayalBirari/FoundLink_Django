About FoundLink

Making lost and found easier.

FoundLink provides one organised platform where students can report lost belongings, share found items and check for possible matches.

01

Easy Reporting

Add the item name, category, location, date and other useful details to create a lost or found report.

02

Smart Matching

FoundLink can highlight possible matches between lost and found reports using details such as item names and locations.

03

Notifications

Check your notifications to stay informed about possible matches and relevant activity on the platform.

04

Student Connections

Connect with people in the community who may be able to provide information about a lost or found item.

05

Manage Reports

Keep track of your submitted lost and found reports and review their current status from your account.

06

AI Assistant

Use the FoundLink assistant for guidance about reporting lost items and using the platform.
